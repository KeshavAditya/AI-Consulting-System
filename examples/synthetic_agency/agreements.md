# Fictional agreements

All organizations, agreements, and events below were authored for this dataset. These are simplified test inputs, not legal templates or real contracts. All amounts are USD and exclude tax; supplied invoice tax is zero. Each agreement was approved on 2026-09-01. No subsequent fee/rate changes were agreed except the explicit P05 credit.

## p01

Client C01 commissions website maintenance at 100.00 per approved billable hour. Work is reviewed and invoiced weekly. The project budget is 40 hours and 2200.00 direct cost, including 200.00 of project-specific infrastructure that is not separately billable. All work is accepted on September 7. There is no hourly cap or discount. Invoice I01 is due September 22.

## p02

Client C02 commissions integration delivery at 120.00 per approved billable hour, billed weekly after agency review. The budget is 50 hours and 3000.00 direct cost; there is no contractual cap on the agreed 50-hour work package. All 50 hours are accepted by September 10. No discount, goodwill write-off, or separate invoice is authorized for the ten omitted hours. Invoice I02 is due September 25. Additional eligible work should be reviewed before a new invoice is prepared; it is not an overdue invoice merely because it was omitted.

## p03

Client C03 commissions accessibility remediation at 150.00 per approved billable hour. Thirty hours are accepted on September 7. The agreed direct cost budget is 2100.00. Billing is performed in a separate accounting system; the time tracker need not contain the invoice or an updated billed flag. Invoice EXT-303 (canonical I03) is due September 22. Duplicate billing is prohibited.

## p04

Client C04 commissions a portal for a fixed fee of 20000.00, payable in an 8000.00 commencement installment and a 12000.00 acceptance installment. The original delivery budget is 200 hours and 10000.00 direct cost. The fixed fee includes implementation, design, and two revision rounds. Additional fees require a written client-approved change describing both the added work and its price; internal timesheet approval is insufficient.

The commencement installment is invoiced September 1 and due September 8. The portal is accepted September 28; the acceptance installment is invoiced that day and due October 12. No approved change order is present. The records do not establish whether later revisions were client additions, corrections, or work already included in scope.

## p05

Client C05 commissions analytics integration at 200.00 per approved billable hour. The agreed work package is 50 hours, accepted September 9, with a direct cost budget of 4000.00. Invoice I05 is issued September 10 and due September 15. On September 25, the parties agree a 1000.00 pre-tax goodwill reduction of this project's price, documented by CR01. It is a price reduction, not cash received or an authorization for more work.
